## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does TimeTrails track my location all the time?
Recording is in your hands and can be paused anytime. The app uses the system's location services to record trails in the background; by default, data is saved only on your device unless you actively enable iCloud Backup.

### What permissions are needed?
Background recording requires "Location (Always + Precise)"; "Motion & Fitness" is used for more accurate step counts and activity recognition; "Notifications" is used for reminders (optional); and "Photos" is only needed when you import photos. Everything except location is optional.

### My location isn't being recorded?
- Go to Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → TimeTrails → select "Always," and turn on "Precise Location."
- Enable "Background App Refresh" for the app.
- "Low Power Mode" may pause background location; try turning it off and retrying.
- Restart your iPhone, then open TimeTrails again.

### It seems to drain the battery?
- The app already uses smart sampling; power usage is lower when the signal is good and you're moving less.
- Keep your system and the app up to date.
- When you stay still for a long time, the sampling frequency is automatically reduced.

### How does iCloud Backup work? How do I restore after switching devices?
Once enabled, your trails are backed up incrementally by day to your own iCloud private space (CloudKit). After switching or reinstalling on a device, when local data is empty, the app will prompt you to "Restore from iCloud"; you can also manually "Sync Now" under "iCloud Data Backup → Data Sync," or use "Full Sync" in the top-right corner to ensure the cloud and your device are consistent. Prerequisites: all devices are signed in to the same Apple ID, iCloud is enabled, the network is working, and the app is kept in the foreground during syncing.

### What happens when I "delete" a trail point?
Deleting is a "hide" rather than a physical erase: the deleted point is no longer displayed, is excluded from statistics and the fog map, and this hidden state syncs to your other devices via iCloud, so it won't "come back" after being deleted.

### How do I completely clear my data?
You can clear on-device data in the app's settings. If iCloud Backup is enabled, please also delete the cloud backup under "iCloud Data Backup"; you'll need to clean up accordingly on other devices signed in to the same Apple ID as well.
