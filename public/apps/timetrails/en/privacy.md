# TimeTrails Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 9, 2026 · Applies to the iOS app "TimeTrails"

Your trails belong to you alone.

## Overview
TimeTrails is a privacy-first trail recording app. Our core principle is simple: by default, your data stays only on your own device. The app requires no account, includes no ads, uses no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs, and will never sell or share your data with any third party.

## What Information We Collect
To record and review your trails, the app collects and generates the following data on your device after you grant permission:

- Location data: To continuously record your trails in the background, the app needs "precise location" and "always" location permissions. Location data is used to draw trails, reveal the fog map, calculate distance, and more.
- Motion and fitness data: Such as step count and activity type, used to more accurately distinguish walking, cycling, driving, and so on, and to refine your daily statistics.
- Time and time zone: Each trail point records the time and time zone at the moment of sampling, so days can be split and summarized correctly by "local time."
- Derived data: Frequently visited places, dwell periods, daily summaries, fog exploration areas, and any trail notes you add yourself, all computed on your device from the raw data above.
- Photos (optional): The photo library is accessed only when you actively import photos to associate with a trail.

The app does not collect identifying information such as your name, phone number, or contacts, and it does not require you to create an account.

## Where Your Data Is Stored
By default, all of the data above is stored only locally on your device (in an on-device database) and is not uploaded to our servers. In fact, this app has no server for storing your personal data.

## iCloud Backup and Sync (Optional, Off by Default)
You can choose to enable "iCloud Data Backup." Once enabled:

- Your data is backed up only to your own iCloud private database (Apple CloudKit Private Database), tied to your Apple ID.
- Only you (devices signed in to the same Apple ID) can access this data; we cannot access it, and the data never passes through any of our servers.
- Its purpose is to let you restore your data after switching or reinstalling on a device, or to sync across your own multiple devices.
- You can turn this feature off at any time, or delete the cloud backup within the app. Use of iCloud is also subject to Apple's Privacy Policy.

## Place Name Resolution (Reverse Geocoding)
To display coordinates as readable place names, the app may send the relevant coordinates to Apple's map/geocoding services to obtain place names. This process is provided by the system and is subject to Apple's Privacy Policy; we do not thereby store or forward your coordinates to any third party.

## Network Requests
The app may make a small number of network requests unrelated to your personal data, such as checking for new versions. These requests do not contain your trails or identifying information.

## What We Will Not Do
- No advertising and no ad SDKs.
- No third-party analytics or user behavior tracking.
- No building of user profiles.
- No selling, renting, or sharing of your personal data.

## Permissions Explained
- Location: The core permission for recording trails in the background ("Always + Precise" recommended).
- Motion and Fitness: Used for step counting and activity recognition.
- Notifications: Used for reminders and the like (optional).
- Photos: Used only when you import photos (optional).

Apart from location, all other permissions are optional; you can adjust or revoke any permission at any time in the system Settings.

## Your Controls
- Pause or resume trail recording at any time.
- "Deleting" a trail point is a hide action: the point is no longer displayed, is excluded from statistics and the fog map, and this state syncs to your other devices via iCloud.
- You can clear on-device data within the app; if iCloud Backup is enabled, you can delete the cloud backup as well.
- You can revoke any permission in the system Settings.

## Data Retention and Deletion
Your data remains on your device (and in the iCloud private space you choose to enable) until you delete it or uninstall the app. Uninstalling the app removes the on-device data; if you previously enabled iCloud Backup, the cloud copy must be deleted separately by you, either within the app or through iCloud.

## Children's Privacy
This app is not designed for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to This Policy
If this policy is materially updated, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, notify you within the app.
