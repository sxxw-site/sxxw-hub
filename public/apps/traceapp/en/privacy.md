# TraceApp Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2025-10-12

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile application services provided by TraceApp (hereinafter referred to as "this App"). We take the protection of your personal information and privacy seriously, and we aim to explain clearly and readably: what information we collect; how we use, store, and protect this information; and what rights you have and how to exercise them.

When you download, install, or use this App, you are deemed to have read and agreed to the terms of this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please stop using and uninstall this App.

## 1. Information We Collect and Use
To deliver its core functions (recording and displaying your trips), this App processes only the following types of information locally on your device:

- Location data (required): Used to record and display your routes, distance, speed, and more. Sourced from iOS CoreLocation and collected only after you grant location permission.
- Motion / activity data such as walking and running (optional): Used to calculate step count, activity type, and more. Sourced from CoreMotion (where applicable).
- Network connection status (for prompts only): Used to determine whether online maps or syncing are available. Does not include any content data.

We do not collect: account information, contacts, SMS/call records, photo library content, microphone/camera data, advertising identifiers (IDFA), or any tracking identifiers that could be used to serve ads. We also do not embed any third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

## 2. Storage and Transmission of Information
- Local storage: Your tracks and related statistics are stored by default in your device's local app sandbox.
- iCloud (if you enable it): Once you turn on iCloud sync, the relevant data may sync across your devices via iCloud. iCloud is provided and protected by Apple; we do not have access to your iCloud credentials or content.
- No upload to our own servers: Unless a cloud service is added in a future version and explicitly authorized by you, this App does not upload your data to the developer's own servers.

## 3. How We Use Information
- To provide features such as trip tracking, distance/pace statistics, track replay, and visualization;
- To provide you with basic network status prompts and error diagnostics (no content is uploaded).

## 4. Permissions and How to Revoke Them (iOS)
- Location Services: Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → TraceApp, where you can choose "While Using the App" or "Always," and turn it off at any time.
- Motion & Fitness: Settings → Privacy & Security → Motion & Fitness → TraceApp, where you can revoke the permission at any time.
- Cellular/Wi-Fi: Settings → TraceApp, where you can turn off "Wireless Data (Wi-Fi & Cellular)."

After you turn off the relevant permissions, some features may become unavailable, but this does not affect your continued use of other features that do not depend on those permissions.

## 5. Your Rights
- Access and export: You can view your track history within the App; to export it, you can use the system share feature (if available) to export images/files.
- Correction: You can delete incorrect records and record again; we do not process or alter your data.
- Deletion: You can delete individual records or all records within the App; uninstalling the App will delete local data. If you use iCloud, synced data must be managed and deleted separately in your iCloud settings.
- Withdrawing consent: Turn off the relevant permissions in iOS Settings, or stop using / uninstall this App.

## 6. Data Security
- Local data is protected by the iOS sandbox mechanism;
- The security of iCloud transmission/storage is provided by Apple (see Apple's privacy documentation for details).

## 7. Children's Privacy
This App is intended for the general public and does not provide services to minors aged 14 or under. If you believe we have collected a minor's information without guardian consent, please contact flywithbug@gmail.com so we can address it.

## 8. Third Parties and Sharing
- The current version does not integrate any third-party advertising/statistics/analytics SDKs, and does not share your personal information with any third party.
- If third-party services are added in the future, we will update this policy, provide a prominent notice within the App, and obtain your explicit consent before enabling them.

## 9. Changes to This Policy
We may revise this policy in response to changes in features or regulations. If a change may materially affect your rights, we will provide a prominent notice within the App or notify you by other reasonable means. The revised version takes effect upon publication; if you do not agree, you may stop using and uninstall the App.
