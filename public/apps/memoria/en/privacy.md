# 拾忆 · Memoria Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 15, 2026

拾忆 · Memoria (hereinafter the "App") is a tool for recording birthdays, anniversaries, and important days. We take your privacy very seriously. The App has no accounts and no servers, and it does not collect or upload any of your personal information to us.

## 1. What Information We Collect
We do not collect any personal information. The content you create in the App (such as names, dates, notes, and colors) is stored only locally on your device.

## 2. iCloud Sync
If you are signed in to iCloud and have enabled sync, your data is synced across your own iPhone, iPad, and Apple Watch through Apple's CloudKit private database. This data is stored in your personal iCloud account, protected by Apple's privacy policy, and the developer cannot access, read, or obtain any of it.

## 3. Face ID and Passcode
If you enable the app lock, the App uses the system's local authentication (Face ID / Touch ID / device passcode) to unlock. The related biometric data is processed locally on your device by the system; the App never accesses, stores, or transmits any biometric information.

## 4. Notifications and Reminders
The App uses local notifications to remind you of anniversaries at the times you set. Notification content is generated only on your device and does not pass through any server.

## 5. Third Parties and Tracking
The App contains no advertising, third-party analytics, event tracking, or tracking SDKs, and does not share data with any third party.

## 6. Data Retention and Deletion
Your data is entirely under your control. You can edit or delete records within the App at any time; to delete data in iCloud, manage it under "Settings → iCloud" on your device. Uninstalling the App deletes the local data.

## 7. Children's Privacy
The App does not target children under the age of 13 for information collection; because we do not collect any personal data at all, it is suitable for users of all ages.

## 8. Changes to This Policy
If this policy is updated, we will publish the latest version on this page and update the effective date.

## 9. App Distribution Platforms

The App is available through the Apple App Store (display name “拾忆 · Memoria”) and Huawei AppGallery (display name “拾忆”). The stores may process information related to downloads, accounts, purchases, or reviews under their own privacy policies; that information is not collected or controlled by the App. Except for iCloud sync on Apple devices as described in Section 2, the App does not transmit your names, dates, notes, or other in-app records to Apple or Huawei.
