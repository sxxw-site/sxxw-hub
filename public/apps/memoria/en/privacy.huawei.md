# Memoria · 拾忆 — Huawei AppGallery Privacy Policy

**Effective date: August 14, 2026**

Memoria · 拾忆 (hereinafter "the App") is a local tool for recording birthdays, anniversaries, and important dates. We take your privacy seriously. **The App has no accounts, no servers, no network access, and does not collect or upload any of your personal information.**

> Note: The App is fully offline. iCloud and cloud sync exist only in the App Store version of Memoria · 拾忆; the HarmonyOS version provides **no** cloud or network capability whatsoever.

## 1. What we collect

**We collect no personal information.** Content you create in the App (event names, dates, notes, colors, reminder settings, etc.) is stored **only on your device**, in the app sandbox via HarmonyOS local storage (RelationalStore and Preferences). This data never leaves your device, and neither we nor any third party can access it.

## 2. Permissions

The App requests only the following permission, used solely for its stated purpose:

| Permission | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Notifications / Publish reminder agent (`ohos.permission.PUBLISH_AGENT_REMINDER`) | To deliver anniversary/birthday reminders locally at the times you set. Reminder content is generated and shown only on your device and is never uploaded. |

You may disable notifications in system settings at any time; doing so affects only reminders.

## 3. What we do not do

- No third-party SDKs, analytics, advertising, or push services;
- No collection of device identifiers, location, contacts, or photos;
- No sharing, selling, or transfer of your data to any third party;
- No network requests of any kind.

## 4. Data storage and security

All your data is stored only in the app sandbox on your device, protected by the system. You can delete data by removing events in-app (and clearing them from the Recycle Bin), or by uninstalling the App, which deletes all local data it created. Because data is local only and not backed up over any network, uninstalling or switching devices will lose the data.

## 5. Children

The App collects no personal information, from anyone including children.

## 6. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
