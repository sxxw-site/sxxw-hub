## Frequently Asked Questions

### Will my data sync to other devices?
Yes. Once you sign in to iCloud and enable sync, your data automatically syncs across iPhone / iPad / Apple Watch through your private iCloud library.

### Is my data secure?
Yes. The App has no accounts, no servers, and no ads or tracking. Your data stays only on your device and in your personal iCloud, and the developer cannot access it. See the Privacy Policy on this site for details.

### Does it support the lunar calendar?
Yes. Both birthdays and anniversaries can use the lunar calendar, which automatically calculates the next Gregorian date.
